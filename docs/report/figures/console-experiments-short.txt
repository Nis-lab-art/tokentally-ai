== Nishan Giri
spell checking: best 5 suggestions for a typo (4713 known words)
      heap (ours)                          1248
      collect and sort all                 1138   (microseconds per search)
      about the same. Why: with only a few hundred candidates a plain sort is just as quick. The heap pays off when there are far more candidates than the 5 we keep.
spell checking: edit distance for words of 8 and 32 letters
      8 letters                             529
      32 letters                           3713   (nanoseconds per comparison)
      16 letters took 1213 ns. 8 to 16 letters costs 2.3x and 8 to 32 letters costs 7.0x, about the 4x and 16x of an a x b table.
cost calculator: price 194 model prices and take the cheapest 10
      top 10 from the heap                48.57
      all models, sorted                   81.5   (microseconds per request)
      top 10 from the heap is 1.7x faster. Why: only 194 prices, so sorting all of them is as quick as the heap. With thousands of models the heap would win.
